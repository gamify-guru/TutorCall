      Detective's handbook

      When vs. while

      Two small words, one big difference. Learn this and Alex will talk.

      while

      A longer action, a background, or two actions at the same time.

      while + was/were + -ing

      • While I was walking home, I called my friend.
      • I was reading a book while I was drinking coffee.

      Ask: What were you doing while you were sitting at the table?

      when

      A short action, an exact moment, or one action after another.

      when + past simple

      • When I left the café, it started raining.
      • When Emma brought the bill, I paid.

      Ask: What happened when you left the café?

      Common mistakes

      Alex replies “I don’t understand the question. Please rephrase.” to all of these.
