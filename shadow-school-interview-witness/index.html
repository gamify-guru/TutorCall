C. Build your report

Agnes's rule cards — Question tags

Form

Positive ➜ negative tag: You can explain it, can't you?
Negative ➜ positive tag: You weren't honest, were you?

Helpers

Use the same helper: is / are / was / were, can, have. No helper? Use do / does / did: You left it, didn't you?

Intonation

Falling ↘ — you check a fact (blue). Rising ↗ — a real question (amber).

Special

I am ➜ aren't I?
Let's ➜ shall we?
There was… ➜ wasn't there?
