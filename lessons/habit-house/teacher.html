About the lesson

Academy Stars 3, Unit 2 revision. Age 11–12. Level A1+/A2. One student online, or project the screen in class. British English: Mum, film, wash up, tidy up.

Aim

Chores, free-time activities, Present Simple, frequency adverbs and expressions.

Time — 40–45 minutes

  • Opening story: 2–3 min
  • Choose 3 missions: 2–3 min
  • Each mission: 8–10 min
  • Final door: 5–6 min
  • Bonus, fast finishers only: about 8 min

Choice rule

Exactly three tiles. At least one Write or Speak. The app orders them Read, then Write, then Speak.

Support

Grammar Rules stays in the header. Feedback is immediate. Speaking works with a microphone or by typing. After two misses, a model appears. Word chips help with spelling. The opening story and some mission texts can be read aloud by the browser (British English, no audio file was added).

Success

The door opens when three code parts are placed (SUN, then 3, then LEAF) and five true sentences pass. The bonus star is extra.

A useful path

Sunny Schedule (read) → Flower Questions (write) → Leaf Talk (speak).

Progress is saved in the browser under one key. Use «↺ Начать заново» to clear it — the lesson asks first.

Nine missions (8 items each)

Read

  • Star Sorter — choose the sentence with the adverb in the right place.
  • Heart Habits — Hart family gap-fill (always, usually, often…).
  • Sunny Schedule — match clues to expressions (every day, twice a week…).
  • Daily Stars — Sam’s diary, adverbs and expressions mixed.

Write / Speak

  • Music & Moves — eight true sentences about free time.
  • Flower Questions — eight How often questions (do / does).
  • Cloud Compare — more often / less often than.
  • Leaf Talk — answer How often questions (speak or type).
  • Smile Stories — eight true sentences, a set frequency word each time.

There is no homework section in the original lesson, so none was added.

Final door — five true sentences

The student writes about their own week. Needed:

  1. A chore + frequency adverb (always, usually, often, sometimes, never, hardly ever).
  2. A free-time activity + frequency adverb.
  3. An expression: every day, once a week, twice a week, twice a day or three times a month…
  4. am / is / are + adverb after it (I am usually tired…).
  5. A How often question and an answer in the same box.

Place the three collected parts on the door in order: SUN, then 3, then LEAF.
