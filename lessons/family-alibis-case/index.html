Grammar HQ — was / were

Where

Where + was / were + person + (place)?
Where was Aunt Sally on Saturday?

When

When + was / were + person + there?
When were you at the lake?

Who

Who + was / were + person + with?
Who was Uncle Bob with?

Weather

What was the weather like + (time)?
It was sunny and windy.

was — I, he, she, it. were — you, we, they.

Time phrases: at two o'clock, on Saturday, in the morning / afternoon.
