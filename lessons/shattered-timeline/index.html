                      Grammar Cards

                      Find the time clue first. Then look at who the sentence is about.

                      01 / Present Simple of to be

                      Use am / is / are for where someone is today or now.

                      • I am at the café today.
                      • Mia is at the library today.
                      • They are at the swimming pool now.

                      02 / Past Simple of to be

                      Use was / were for where someone was yesterday or last Saturday.

                      • Mia was at the cinema yesterday.
                      • I was at the train station yesterday.
                      • They were at the shopping centre last Saturday.

                      03 / Today vs Yesterday

                      Yesterday / last SaturdayToday / now
                      She was at the cinema.She is at the library.
                      They were at the shopping centre.They are at the swimming pool.
