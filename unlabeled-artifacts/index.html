museum

/mjuːˈziː.əm/ • noun

A building where people can see important objects connected with art, history, or science.

💬 "We are visiting the history museum today."

history

/ˈhɪs.tər.i/ • noun

The study of things that happened in the past, including prehistoric times.

💬 "In history class, we study how early humans lived."
🔥

fire

/faɪər/ • noun

Heat and bright light from burning wood. It was hot and gave light and warmth.

💬 "Fire was for keeping warm and cooking food."
🪓

tool

/tuːl/ • noun

An instrument you hold in your hand and use to make or repair things or build shelters.

💬 "A stone axe was an ancient tool for making things."

sculpture

/ˈskʌlp.tʃər/ • noun

💬 "The stone head in the glass showcase is a sculpture."

Grammar Focus: What was it for?

In Academy Stars 3 Unit 8, we ask about the purpose of ancient artifacts using the past tense pattern:

Pattern 1: for + -ing verb
"What was it for?"
→ "It was for wearing around the neck."
→ "It was for cutting meat."
Pattern 2: used to + verb
"What did they use it for?"
→ "People used it to make tools."
→ "They used it to cook food."

Detective Field Tips

  • Look closely at materials: Prehistoric people used stone, clay, wood, animal bone, and early metals (gold and bronze).
  • Check spelling carefully: Notice silent letters like the k in knife and the ending -ture in sculpture.
  • Compound words: Musical instrument is two words! Don't forget the space.
