Start the Adventure

The Seasonal Guardian

“Welcome, Harvest Hero! My farm is full of food — apples on trees, grapes on vines, potatoes in the ground. But the Harvest Feast gates will not open until you show me what you know. Complete any 3 missions to win 3 Keys of Growth. At least one mission must be speaking or writing. Then pass my final challenge and invite me to the feast!”

How the lesson works

3 Keys of Growth
9 Missions to choose
6–8 Sentences to say
  1. Choose 3 missions. Sorting, reading, speaking, writing, matching and more.
  2. Guardian Challenge. Speak or write 6–8 sentences and invite the Guardian.
  3. Celebrate! Open the gates and get your Harvest Hero certificate.

All missions, the Guardian and the feast are open now — go in any order. Progress saves on this device.

Quick check

Missions

Nine missions on the farm

Choose any three to earn Keys of Growth. At least one should be speaking or writing (marked ✏️). Everything is open — play more if you like!

01 · Sorting · 5 min

Sort the Harvest

Tap a food, then tap the right basket. Put 8 foods in the right place: on a tree, on a vine, on a bush, in the ground.

02 · Reading · 6 min

How Oranges Grow

Read a short story about seeds, flowers and bees. Then answer 6 questions.

03 · Comparing + speaking · 6 min ✏️

Potato vs Watermelon

Compare a potato and a watermelon. Choose 6 true sentences, listen, and say them aloud.

04 · Writing · 7 min ✏️

Would You Like…?

Build 8 sentences. Tap the correct words, then listen and say them. Write one extra sentence of your own.

Bonus: write your own sentence

05 · Speaking dialogue · 7 min ✏️

Market Role-Play

You are at the farm market. Choose the best reply for each of the 8 lines.

06 · Creating + writing · 7 min ✏️

Party Invitation

Build your Harvest Feast invitation. Choose at least 4 parts, write your own line, then read it aloud.

07 · Matching · 5 min

Match Farm Activities

Tap a sentence on the left, then tap the matching activity on the right. Match all 8 pairs.

08 · Drag & drop · 5 min

Where Does It Grow?

Drag a food to a basket — or tap a food, then tap a basket. 8 foods!

09 · Gap-fill · 5 min

At the Harvest Feast

Complete 8 sentences. Choose a, an or some — and Yes, please / No, thanks.

Final Challenge

Invite the Guardian!

This section is always open. Speak or write 6–8 sentences. You must: name food, say how it grows, and invite the Guardian.
