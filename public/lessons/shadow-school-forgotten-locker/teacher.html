Для учителя: Забытый шкафчик — Past Perfect

Эта страница не связана с уроком для ученика. Домашнего задания в оригинале не было — я его не добавляла. Аудиофайла не присылали: в досье лексики работает британский голос браузера (en-GB), потому что в оригинале было обещано «услышь шёпот», но звука там не было.

Цель

Различать более раннее и более позднее действие в прошлом: Past Perfect (had + V₃) и Past Simple. Маркеры: before, after, by the time, already, never, ever, when. Лексика крыла: locker, vanish, curse, secret, diary, century, key, warning, trace, abandoned.

Ключи

1. Хронология исчезновения

  1. Lily had discovered the cursed diary before she hid the key in the locker.
  2. She had written a warning on the last page after she had realized the truth about the curse.
  3. The librarian locked the abandoned wing when she noticed that Lily was gone.
  4. Before anyone searched the corridor the ghost had already vanished without a trace.
  5. The key had been hidden for fifty years until we opened the locker.
  6. Lily had prepared everything before midnight and she had already left by the time dawn came.
  7. The curse had begun long before Lily arrived and it had affected every student since the 19th century.
  8. Nobody had seen the warning until we found the diary.

2. Загадка дневника

  1. I had already read the old warning before I decided to open the locker.
  2. By the time the moon rose, I had hidden the key behind the diary.
  3. Before the curse took effect, Lily had already prepared a secret passage.
  4. The ghost vanished after it had delivered its final warning.
  5. No one had ever seen the abandoned wing before we found the old key.
  6. She had written a secret note before the door locked forever.
  7. The century-old curse had begun before anyone realized its power.

В оригинале у пункта 4 оба пропуска стояли как Past Perfect и правильный вариант был первым. Я поставила учебную пару: vanished (позже) + had delivered (раньше).

3. Поиск улик

  1. Lily had hidden the key before she vanished.
  2. We entered the corridor before the moon had risen.
  3. She had warned everyone about the curse before it began.
  4. The ghost had already found the diary when we arrived.
  5. They had locked the wing before they found any evidence.
  6. The school had stood for a century before it happened.

В оригинале «правильный порядок» был самим перемешанным массивом слов, фразы получались обрывистыми, проверка сравнивала склейку без пробелов. Я собрала полные грамматичные предложения по подсказкам.

4. Исправление записок

  1. I had already left the room before I found the key.
  2. The ghost had vanished before dawn arrived.
  3. She hid the key after she had hidden the diary.
  4. By the time we arrived, the locker had already been locked.
  5. The warning had been written in the diary before the curse began.
  6. No one had ever seen the abandoned wing until we opened it.
  7. Lily had already gone before we searched the corridor.
  8. The curse had begun before Lily arrived at the school, and she had already been aware.

5. Хроника интерната

  1. opened
  2. arrived / had already established
  3. began / had stolen
  4. enrolled / had already heard
  5. had found / stopped
  6. locked / had vanished
  7. had entered / discovered
  8. opened … found

В оригинале у пунктов 3, 5 и 6 правильный ответ часто стоял первым и не всегда следовал правилу «раньше — Past Perfect». Я выровняла ключи под правило урока.
